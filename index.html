GLP-1 · Semaglutide

Behind the first generic semaglutide approvals

Neo's spring-driven mechanism delivers a consistent dose regardless of patient strength or dexterity, while Harmony 2 keeps dial extension low even at high doses — together powering first approvals in Canada and the first US FDA tentative approval.

Explore ShailyPen Neo™
Sustainability by design

A weekly therapy, years of use, far less waste

Toby™ Reusable puts the pre-filled syringe in a single-use Syringe Pod that clips to a retained driver — significantly reducing material, energy, shipping and cold-chain demands compared with a fully disposable auto-injector.

Explore Toby™ Reusable
Large-volume biologics

From infusion clinic to home

Mira™ attaches to the patient and delivers subcutaneous therapies up to 20 ml, with delivery programmable from one minute to two hours — letting large-volume biologics be self-administered at home.
